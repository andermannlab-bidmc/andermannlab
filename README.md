## How to make common changes
Open the **Edit the website** link, sign in with GitHub, then:

- **Add a new lab member:** People → Current lab members → **Add an entry** → type name, title, email, pick their row, upload a photo → **Save**.
- **Someone left the lab:** People → delete their card under *Current lab members* → under *Previous lab members* click **Add an entry**, type their name and pick their role → **Save**.
- **Add a paper or preprint:** Publications → **Add an entry** → year, paste the citation, paste the link → **Save**.
  (Published papers from PubMed are added automatically every Monday — you only need this for preprints.)
- **Change Research / Home / Contact text:** "Home, Research & Contact text" → edit → **Save**.

The live site updates about a minute after you save. Everything is sorted and laid out automatically.
